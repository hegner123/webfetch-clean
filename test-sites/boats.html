WELCOME TO PREMIER WATERSPORTS!

Premier Watersports is Tennessee’s dealer for Supra Wakeboard Boats, Moomba Boats, Yamaha Sport Boats, Starcraft Boats and Sylvan Pontoons, with locations in Knoxville and Nashville. We keep a large inventory of top-quality pre-owned boats of all types. Each Premier Watersports location also boasts a complete pro shop, featuring skis and wakeboards and accessories, tubes, surfboards, kneeboards and just about everything else you could possibly need for a great weekend on the lake.
